Privacy Policy  ·  Effective Date: October 21, 2025

👋 Introduction

Auto SMS Forwarder ("the App") is developed and maintained by S.M. Shahriyar. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and what we do — and do not — do with it.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

📩 SMS Data Sensitive

The App reads incoming SMS messages solely to forward them to the destination(s) you configure (phone number, REST API, or Google Sheet).

We never store, upload, or share your SMS content with any third-party server, analytics service, or advertiser. All processing happens entirely on your device.

🔐 Permissions Used

  • RECEIVE_SMS / READ_SMS – to detect and read incoming messages for forwarding.
  • SEND_SMS – to forward messages to a configured phone number.
  • INTERNET – to forward messages to a REST API or Google Sheet endpoint you provide.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED – to restart the forwarding service automatically after a device reboot.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / POST_NOTIFICATIONS – to keep the background service alive and show a persistent status notification.
  • REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS – to prevent Android from killing the service during idle periods.
No permission is used for advertising, tracking, or any purpose beyond core SMS forwarding functionality.

💾 Data Storage

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your forwarding configuration (destination numbers, API URL, filters).
  • A local log of forwarded messages for your own review.
  • App settings (enable/disable toggles, blocked senders, etc.).

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly configure a forwarding destination.

🌐 Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, crash reporting, advertising SDKs, or tracking libraries.

If you configure an external REST API or Google Sheets webhook as a forwarding destination, your message content will be sent to that endpoint. The privacy practices of those services are governed by their own policies.

👶 Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
